About Nicholas Korom

Built through experience.
Focused on execution.

Entrepreneur, real estate investor, and founder of BNB Accelerator.

My story

From building a business to building an acquisition process.

I began investing in real estate in 2019. After building and exiting Patient Rhino in 2022, I focused on short-term rentals and the work that goes into selecting, buying, and preparing a property for guests.

BNB Accelerator grew from that experience. The team brings sourcing, transaction coordination, and launch support into a connected process, with the client approving the property and the important decisions along the way.

For me, the strongest conversations start with the details: the property, the assumptions, the responsibilities, and the plan after closing.

Operating principles

The work behind a good decision.

01

Make assumptions visible

Revenue, financing, operating costs, and reserves should be clear enough to question.

02

Keep ownership personal

Each investor has different capital, goals, and responsibilities. The purchase needs to fit those facts.

03

Plan beyond closing

Guest experience, furnishing, local operations, and the launch handoff belong in the acquisition conversation.
