About Nicholas Korom

Serial Entrepreneur, Real‑Estate Investor & Cao of My Bnb Accelerator, Specializing in Cash‑Flowing Airbnb Short‑Term Rentals & Tax‑Efficient Real‑Estate Investing.

Nicholas Korom is an entrepreneur at heart. He builds businesses from the ground up and acquires real‑estate portfolios that deliver both cash flow and tax benefits. His mission: serve high‑income earners who want to invest in short‑term rental real estate without doing the heavy lifting themselves.

Building Real‑Estate Portfolios from Acquisition to Exit

Nicholas Korom has mastered the process of identifying short‑term rental properties, structuring low‑money‑down deals, and optimizing them for cash flow and tax savings. After exiting his last business in 2022 for a multi‑7‑figure return, he shifted focus to real‑estate — acquiring his first properties in 2019 and exclusively scaling into Airbnb short‑term rentals from 2021‑2025.

Advising High‑Income Earners to Invest with Minimum Effort

Nick leads a team of 15 full‑time and dozens of part‑time team members and contractors at My BnB Accelerator. He has helped hundreds of clients close deals each year, with one out of every two clients referring another within 9 months, and clients typically doing their second deal with him in under 10 months. His process is nearly 95% done‑for‑you and built for those who don’t have the time or niche expertise to execute on their own.

Nicholas Korom’s Story

Nicholas began his career by building and selling a company (Patient Rhino, LLC) for a multi‑7‑figure exit in 2022. He then pivoted to real‑estate investing, learning early lessons from long‑term rentals before discovering the tax and cash‑flow benefits of short‑term rentals. Today he leads My BnB Accelerator in Denver, Colorado, helping high‑income earners invest in properties they own, manage and profit from — all while reducing their tax burden.
