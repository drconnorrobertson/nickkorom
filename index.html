About

Taking the Guesswork Out of STR Investing

Nicholas Korom is the founder and Chief Accelerating Officer of My BnB Accelerator, LLC, a done-for-you short-term rental acquisition and management service built exclusively for high-income earners making over $500,000 per year.

With over 500 homes closed for more than 300 clients across the United States, Nick has built a reputation for identifying cash-flowing properties, negotiating aggressively on behalf of his clients, and connecting them with the right tax advisors, designers, and property managers to keep the entire process hands-off.

His proprietary approach, The Reverse Offset Method, combines real estate acquisition with strategic tax planning, helping clients offset significant portions of their W-2 or business income through properly structured short-term rental investments.
