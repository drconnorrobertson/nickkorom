Meet Nick Korom

Nick Korom is the founder and Chief Accelerating Officer of BNB Accelerator, a done-for-you short-term rental acquisition company.

His work focuses on building a repeatable process for selecting markets, sourcing properties, coordinating due diligence, negotiating acquisitions, and launching guest-ready short-term rentals.

Nick's operating philosophy is straightforward: make assumptions visible, define responsibilities clearly, and judge the process by documented client outcomes. Investment, financing, legal, tax, and operating decisions remain with the client and the client's independent advisers.

Nick's Operating Principles

The standards behind the acquisition and launch process at BNB Accelerator.

🔍

Start With Fit

A short-term rental is not appropriate for every investor. Capital, timeline, risk tolerance, financing, and desired involvement should be assessed before a property search begins.

🏠

Make Assumptions Visible

Revenue projections, operating costs, reserves, financing, and regulatory assumptions should be separated so the investor can evaluate the downside as well as the base case.

💰

Buy Deliberately

Property sourcing is followed by client-directed due diligence, inspections, negotiation, and closing. The client owns the property and approves the material decisions.

🔨

Design for the Guest

Renovation, furnishing, and setup choices should serve a defined guest profile and market position, not a generic design formula.

📷

Launch With Accountability

Photography, listing setup, operational handoff, and property management introductions should have clear owners, milestones, and acceptance criteria.

📈

Use Independent Advisers

BNB Accelerator is not a CPA, law firm, lender, or property manager. Clients should rely on qualified independent professionals for tax, legal, financing, and ongoing management advice.

How It Works

A coordinated path from investor fit through a guest-ready property launch.

Fit and Strategy

The team reviews the investor's goals, available capital, timing, market preferences, and operating expectations.

Market & Deal Sourcing

The team narrows the approved markets and sources properties against the client's acquisition criteria.

Acquisition & Setup

The client makes the purchase decision while the team coordinates due diligence, project work, furnishing, and launch milestones.

Launch and Handoff

The property is prepared for launch and transitioned to the client's chosen operating arrangement. Results vary by property and market.
